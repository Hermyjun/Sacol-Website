Our Story

Daily'Sweez Bakery was started because we believe great bread and pastries bring people together. Many stores sell mass-produced food with lots of preservatives, so we wanted to bring back real, scratch-made baking.

We love being part of our community. Our customers include local workers, parents, students, teachers, families, seniors, and kids. Everyone is welcome here!
